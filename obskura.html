The house

OBSKURA makes six frames, 640 pairs to a drop. Each frame is produced until its batch is finished and then they are not produced again. There is no archive sale, no restock and no seasonal apology.

Fronts are milled from solid 7075 or moulded in polyamide. Lenses are chemically hardened mineral glass, 1.2 to 1.6 millimetres, blocking the full ultraviolet range.

Established Barcelona, 2021.
